OUR HISTORY

Discover the story behind El Roble Academy.

Our Story

El Roble Academy, also known as E.R.A., was founded with the purpose of creating an educational community where students could develop their knowledge, talents, and values. From our earliest days, we have worked to build a school based on education, respect, responsibility, and excellence.

Our Journey

How We Started

El Roble Academy began as an educational project with a clear vision: to provide students with a place where they could learn and grow both academically and personally.

Our First Years

During our first years, the E.R.A. community began to grow. Students, teachers, and families came together to build a community based on teamwork, dedication, and a commitment to education.

Growth & Development

Over time, the school continued to develop. New areas, activities, programs, and opportunities were introduced to provide students with a more complete educational experience.

E.R.A. Today

Today, El Roble Academy continues to grow and educate new generations of students. Our community continues working toward excellence and preparing students to face the challenges of the future.

El Roble Academy Today

Today, E.R.A. is an educational community committed to providing students with the tools they need to achieve their goals. Our school combines education, activities, values, teamwork, and opportunities for personal growth to prepare students for the future.
