Our School Campus

El Roble Academy's campus is organized into different areas that allow our students to learn, socialize, practice sports, develop their skills, and enjoy different school activities.

📚 Classrooms

Our classrooms are welcoming spaces designed for learning, participation, and the academic development of our students.

⚽ Sports Area

Our sports areas provide students with opportunities to practice different sports, develop their skills, and learn the importance of teamwork.

🔬 Laboratories

Our laboratories are designed for practical activities where students can explore science, conduct experiments, and discover how the world works.

🌳 Green Areas

Our outdoor green areas provide students with peaceful spaces to relax, socialize, and enjoy recreational activities during the school day.

🍎 Cafeteria

Our cafeteria is a comfortable space where students can enjoy their meals, spend time with friends, and share moments during the school day.

🎭 Auditorium

Our auditorium is a versatile space for events, presentations, cultural activities, meetings, celebrations, and special school programs.

🏫 Administration

The administration area is where different aspects of school life are coordinated and where students and families can receive assistance and support.

A Place to Learn & Grow

Every space on our campus has a purpose: to create an environment where students can learn, connect, discover their talents, and grow as individuals. At E.R.A., our campus is more than a place to study; it is an important part of our community.
